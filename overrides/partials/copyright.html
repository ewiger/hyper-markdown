{#- The site's footer, overriding Material's own `partials/copyright.html`. This site *is* `doc/`, and `doc/` is CC BY 4.0 while the tools it documents are MIT. A visitor quoting the language specification needs both terms on the page they are quoting from, and two licenses do not fit in `copyright:`, which is a single string — hence a template override rather than a longer setting. The `©` line is the attribution string `LICENSE-DOCS` asks for, in the one place a reader quoting a page will look: holder, year, license, link. The holder and the year are copied from the license files rather than derived, and the holder links `CONTRIBUTORS.md` the way they do — `build_date_utc.year` is the build year rather than the year of publication, and would put this line in disagreement with all five of them the moment the year turns. Bump it here when they bump. Three things here are load-bearing, and all three are easy to lose on a Material upgrade. The `md-copyright` and `md-copyright__highlight` classes are what the footer's CSS styles; without them the line inherits nothing. The generator branch is kept verbatim from the partial this replaces, so `extra.generator` still means what it says rather than being silently ignored. And every link here is absolute: the license files and `CONTRIBUTORS.md` live outside `docs_dir` and never reach the built site, so `{{ 'LICENSE' | url }}` would resolve to a page that does not exist. `rel="license"` is the machine-readable half of the attribution CC BY asks for. Re-diff this against `site-packages/material/templates/partials/copyright.html` when the theme is upgraded. -#}
